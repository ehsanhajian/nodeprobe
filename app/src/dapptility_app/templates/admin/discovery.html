How scoring works
Lead score (0–100)

Automated outreach priority. Own-domain RPC (+35), mainnet (+25), HTTPS (+10), domain alignment (+15), new discovery (+20). Known providers like Alchemy (−50) are scored low. Leads ≥ 70 are auto-promoted.

Promote → project

Creates an outbound project with the RPC endpoint pre-filled. You can then run a limited scan, review findings, and draft outreach.

Dismiss

Hides the lead from the inbox. It stays in sync so it won't reappear as new, but won't clutter your queue.
