How this works

Lead score (0–100)

An automated outreach priority score. Higher = more likely a project-run RPC worth contacting. Own-domain RPC (+35), mainnet (+25), HTTPS (+10), and domain alignment (+15) increase the score. Testnets (−25) and known providers like Alchemy (−50) lower it. Scores ≥ 70 are auto-promoted to projects.

Promote

Creates an outbound project with this chain's RPC endpoint pre-filled, so you can run a limited scan, review findings, and draft outreach. Use this when a lead looks worth pursuing manually.

Dismiss

Hides the lead from the New queue without creating a project. Dismissed leads stay in ChainList sync but won't clutter your inbox.
